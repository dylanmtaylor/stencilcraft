# Canvas

Part of the [Cricut Design Space feature library](README.md). Written in our own words from
public sources, retrieved 2026-11-02.

### Canvas layout
- **What**: the design workspace. Design panel on the left (Templates, Projects, Images,
  Text, Shapes, Upload, Monogram Maker, AI Create); the Edit bar above the work area;
  the Layers panel on the right; a Make button to continue to the cut.
- **Sources**: [Canvas screen guide](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/26751604105879-Canvas-screen-Design-Space-Guide).

### Canvas grid and units
- **What**: background grid on or off; Imperial (inches) or Metric (cm).
- **Where**: desktop menu › Settings › Canvas. On iOS/Android, Settings at the bottom of the
  screen. There is also a keyboard shortcut to toggle the grid (see [shortcuts](shortcuts.md)).
- **Sources**: [Design Space Settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023589934-Design-Space-Settings), [Design Space FAQ](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009503353-Design-Space-FAQ).

### Snap to Align (alignment guides)
- **What**: guides that show while you drag, so objects line up with other objects. An
  on/off setting.
- **Where**: Settings › Canvas.
- **Sources**: [Design Space Settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023589934-Design-Space-Settings).

### Smart Guides: equal distance
- **What**: temporary spacing markers while you move an object, so the gaps to its
  neighbours can be made equal. A later fix limits them to objects in the design area.
- **Sources**: [older release notes](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/29247748906519-Older-Design-Space-Release-Notes).

### Operation-type display
- **What**: a Canvas setting that controls how operation types are shown.
- **Where**: Settings › Canvas.
- **Sources**: [Design Space Settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023589934-Design-Space-Settings).

### Light mode
- **What**: switch between a light and a dark interface.
- **Availability**: iOS only, per the help centre.
- **Sources**: [Design Space Settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023589934-Design-Space-Settings).

### Zoom and pan
- **What**: zoom in and out of the Canvas, and pan around it.
- **Sources**: [Canvas screen guide](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/26751604105879-Canvas-screen-Design-Space-Guide).

### Selection
- **What**: click to select. Shift or Ctrl-click adds to the selection (Shift or Cmd on
  Mac). Select all and deselect are on the Edit bar.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space).

### Undo and redo
- **What**: step back and forward through edits. No history panel is documented.
- **Sources**: [Canvas screen guide](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/26751604105879-Canvas-screen-Design-Space-Guide).

### Live preview on real objects
- **What**: the 2026 Design Space update for Joy 2 and Explore 5 lists a live project
  preview on real objects.
- **Sources**: [blog: Joy 2 and Explore 5](https://cricut.com/blog/meet-cricut-joy-2-and-cricut-explore-5/).
