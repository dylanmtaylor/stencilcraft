# Images and upload

Part of the [Cricut Design Space feature library](README.md). Written in our own words from
public sources, retrieved 2026-11-02.

### Images panel
- **What**: search the vendor image library, view bookmarked images, or use popular filters.
- **Options**: Advanced Search at the bottom adds image sets and filters for operation,
  project type, editable images and language. There is also an AI Generated filter.
- **Availability**: free, Cricut Access and purchased images. Offline, search covers only
  downloaded images.
- **Sources**: [Using Images](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009426074-Using-Images-in-Design-Space), [free and purchased content](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009552733-How-to-find-free-and-purchased-content-in-Design-Space).

### Upload panel (v10.8, desktop)
- **What**: a redesigned panel (October 2026) for uploading images, SVGs and other
  supported files. It takes fewer steps, says plainly which formats are supported, and
  gives clearer errors.
- **Sources**: [release notes](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/27054006457111-Design-Space-Release-Notes).

### Supported upload formats
- **Raster**: JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, and HEIC (listed in some articles).
- **Vector**: SVG and DXF. DXF does not work on iOS or Android.
- **Not supported**: TIFF and PDF (stated for mobile). No WebP support was found.
- **Limits**: an SVG may have at most 5,000 paths. Vector files with pattern fills,
  editable text or linked images upload with errors or lose that content.
- **Sources**: [upload images](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009556313-How-to-upload-images-into-Design-Space), [unsupported items](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009553213-Image-uploads-unsupported-items).

### Automatic background remover
- **What**: one-click removal of a photo's background.
- **Availability**: Cricut Access only.
- **Sources**: [upload images](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009556313-How-to-upload-images-into-Design-Space).

### Manual cleanup: Select, Erase, Restore
- **What**: Select removes connected areas of the same colour. Erase and Restore are brushes
  that remove or bring back parts of the image.
- **Options**: Reduce Colors and Color Tolerance. These replaced the old choice of simple,
  moderate or complex image.
- **Availability**: free.
- **Sources**: [upload images](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009556313-How-to-upload-images-into-Design-Space).

### Despeckle and Smooth
- **What**: advanced cleanup tools for uploads.
- **Availability**: iOS only.
- **Sources**: [Design Space FAQ](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009503353-Design-Space-FAQ).

### Preview single layer
- **What**: shows the image as one cut layer, which reveals leftover background specks to
  clean up.
- **Sources**: [upload images](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009556313-How-to-upload-images-into-Design-Space).

### Save as Cut image or Print Then Cut image
- **What**: the last upload step. Choose whether the upload becomes an outline (cut) or a
  printable image with a cut outline (Print Then Cut). Then name it, tag it and upload it
  to your library.
- **Sources**: [upload images](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009556313-How-to-upload-images-into-Design-Space).

### Upload patterns
- **What**: upload your own image to use as a pattern fill on printable layers.
- **Sources**: [upload patterns](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010018093-How-to-upload-Patterns-into-Design-Space).

### Downloading images for offline use
- **What**: download images one at a time from the tile's menu. A computer icon marks
  downloaded images.
- **Limits**: free images work offline for 7 days in a row. Cricut Access images work for
  30 days, or until the subscription renews, whichever comes first.
- **Availability**: desktop and iOS.
- **Sources**: [offline feature](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033894014-Design-Space-Offline-Feature).
