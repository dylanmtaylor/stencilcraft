# Layers panel

Part of the [Cricut Design Space feature library](README.md). Written in our own words from
public sources, retrieved 2026-11-02.

### Group / Ungroup
- **What**: grouped layers move and resize together. Grouping does not change how they cut.
- **Shortcut**: Ctrl+G.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space).

### Duplicate
- **What**: copy and paste in one step. Shortcut Ctrl+D.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space).

### Delete
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space).

### Hide / show layer
- **What**: a hidden layer is not cut, drawn, scored or printed.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space).

### Rename and reorder layers
- **What**: rename a layer, or drag it to change the stacking order.
- **Availability**: desktop and iOS only.
- **Sources**: [move and rename layers](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9505742280727-How-to-move-and-rename-layers-in-the-Layers-panel).

### Slice
- **What**: splits two overlapping layers into three or more new shapes.
- **Rules**: needs exactly two layers selected; ungrouped letters must be welded first.
- **Shortcut**: Shift+Alt+Q.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space).

### Weld
- **What**: permanently joins overlapping layers into one shape and removes the inner cut
  lines. The pieces can no longer be edited separately.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space).

### Combine: Unite, Subtract, Intersect, Exclude
- **What**: non-destructive shape operations. Every part stays editable and each action can
  be changed or undone. While active, the result keeps its colour and shows slightly darker
  with an inner shadow.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space), [older release notes](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/29247748906519-Older-Design-Space-Release-Notes).

### Attach / Detach
- **What**: keeps layers in their Canvas arrangement on the mat. It also fastens Draw,
  Score or Foil layers to the cut layer they belong to. Detach undoes it.
- **Shortcut**: Alt+A.
- **Sources**: [Attach](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009380514-How-to-use-Attach-in-Design-Space).

### Flatten / Unflatten
- **What**: merges the selected layers into one printable layer for Print Then Cut.
  Unflatten splits them again.
- **Shortcut**: Alt+F.
- **Sources**: [Combine, Slice and Weld](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/9503908902551-Using-Combine-Slice-and-Weld-to-create-new-shapes-in-Design-Space), [Print Then Cut](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009387274-How-to-Print-Then-Cut-in-Design-Space).

### Contour
- **What**: hides individual cut lines of an image so they are not cut. Multi-layer images
  must be ungrouped first.
- **Sources**: [Contour](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009508613-How-to-use-Contour-in-Design-Space).

### Color Sync panel
- **What**: lowers the number of materials (mats) a project needs. Drag a layer onto another
  layer to take its colour. Only Cut layers are listed (not draw, score or print).
- **Sources**: [Color Sync panel](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009510453-Design-Space-Color-Sync-Panel).

### Blank Canvas / guide layers
- **What**: Templates appear as Guide layers in the panel and are never sent to the machine.
- **Sources**: [Using Templates](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009548273-Using-Templates-in-Design-Space).
