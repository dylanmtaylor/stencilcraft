# Materials and tools

Part of the [Cricut Design Space feature library](README.md). Written in our own words from
public sources, retrieved 2026-11-02.

### Material list and presets
- **What**: a long list of materials, each with preset pressure, number of passes and the
  blade or tool to use.
- **Sources**: [material settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009501793-How-do-I-choose-material-settings), [which materials](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009504773-Which-materials-can-I-cut-with-my-Cricut-smart-cutting-machine).

### Custom materials
- **What**: add a material or edit one. Options: cut pressure, multi-cut (passes) and blade
  type. Cricut suggests changing pressure in steps of 2–4 and test cutting.
- **Where**: desktop menu › Settings › Machines › Custom Materials.
- **Availability**: cannot be created or edited on Android, but changes made elsewhere show
  there. Unavailable offline on iOS.
- **Sources**: [cut pressure / custom materials](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009383274-How-do-I-change-the-cut-pressure-on-my-machine), [Design Space Settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023589934-Design-Space-Settings).

### Smart Materials (matless cutting)
- **What**: backed materials (vinyl, iron-on, label paper, stencil film and others) that feed
  straight into newer machines without a mat.
- **Minimum length**: 6 in (15 cm) on Explore 3 and Maker 3 and later; 4 in (10 cm) on Joy;
  6 in on Joy Xtra.
- **Maximum**:
  - Explore 3 and Maker 3: 11.7 in wide × 12 ft long.
  - Joy: one shape up to 4.5 in × 4 ft, repeated cuts to 20 ft.
  - Joy Xtra: 8.5 in wide; single images to 48 in, repeated to 20 ft.
  - Venture: single images to 12 ft, repeated to 75 ft, 24 in wide on 25 in rolls.
- **Roller margin**: Joy needs 1 in of space above the design and 0.5 in below for the
  rollers.
- **Sources**: [all about Smart Materials](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500007912041-All-About-Smart-Materials), [Joy loading](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040664714-How-to-load-unload-mats-and-materials-Cricut-Joy-series-machines), [Joy Xtra guide](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/26308155935895-Cricut-Joy-Xtra-Quick-Start-Guide), [Venture guide](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/15863937671319-Cricut-Venture-Quick-Start-Guide).

### Mats
- **What**: 12×12 in and 12×24 in mats in Light, Standard, Strong and Fabric grip for
  Explore and Maker. Joy uses 4.5×6.5 and 4.5×12 mats. There are card mats: Card Mat 2×2,
  Card Mat 1×2, Joy Card Mat and Joy Xtra Card Mat (for R10/R20 insert cards).
- **Max cut on mat**: 11.5×11.5 in on a 12×12 mat; 11.5×23.5 in on a 12×24 mat; Joy 2:
  4.25×6.25 or 4.25×11.75 in.
- **Sources**: [Explore Air 2 guide](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/26296804719511-Cricut-Explore-Air-2-Quick-Start-Guide), [Maker 5 specifications](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/42865107739287-Cricut-Maker-5-specifications-and-requirements), [Joy 2 guide](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/36545520180887-Cricut-Joy-2-Quick-Start-Guide), [Card Mat 2×2](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/4919032163223-Cricut-Card-Mat-2-x-2), [insert cards](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044304673-Cricut-Insert-Cards-and-Card-Mat).

### Blades and tools
| Tool | Use | Machines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fine-Point blade (premium) | general cutting | Explore, Maker |
| Deep-Point blade | thicker materials; own housing | Explore, Maker |
| Bonded-Fabric blade | bonded fabric | Explore, Maker |
| Rotary Blade | fabric and soft materials, Fabric Grip mat, pieces of at least 3/4 in; drive housing | Maker family |
| Knife Blade | balsa, mat board, chipboard (multi-pass); drive housing | Maker family; desktop only |
| Scoring Stylus | fold lines | Explore 4 and earlier, Maker 4 and earlier, Venture (not Maker 5) |
| Scoring Wheel (single, double) | fold lines in light or heavy stock; QuickSwap housing | Maker family |
| Engraving Tip / Engraving Tool | engraving | Maker family |
| Debossing Tip | debossing | Maker family |
| Perforation Blade | tear lines | Maker family |
| Wavy Blade | wavy edges | Maker family |
| Foil Transfer Tool | foil | Explore, Maker; Joy has its own |
| Performance blade | general cutting | Venture, Maker 5 |
| Joy blade, Joy pens | cutting and drawing | Joy family |

- **Notes**: QuickSwap tips share one housing. Maker 5 cannot tell apart tools that lack a
  geared housing, so you pick the tool in Design Space.
- **Sources**: [blades and machine tools](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009432294-All-About-Cricut-Blades-and-Machine-Tools), [Maker 5 tools](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/42954507197463-Cricut-Maker-5-compatible-tool-accessories-and-materials), [Maker 5 FAQ](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/42954702029207-Cricut-Maker-5-frequently-asked-questions), [Design Space FAQ](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009503353-Design-Space-FAQ).
