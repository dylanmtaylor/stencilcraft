# Settings and account

Part of the [Cricut Design Space feature library](README.md). Written in our own words from
public sources, retrieved 2026-11-02.

### Settings › Canvas
- **What**: grid on/off; Imperial or Metric; Light mode (iOS); operation-type display;
  Snap to Align.
- **Sources**: [Design Space Settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023589934-Design-Space-Settings).

### Settings › Machines
- **What**: per machine type, a default load type (on mat / without mat) and a default
  Print Then Cut page size. The Custom Materials list is also here.
- **Sources**: [Design Space Settings](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023589934-Design-Space-Settings).

### Settings › Print Then Cut
- **What**: page size, and calibration on mobile.
- **Sources**: [calibration](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009424974-Calibrating-your-machine-for-Print-Then-Cut).

### Keyboard shortcuts list
- **What**: an in-app list of shortcuts, from Help and from Settings. See [shortcuts](shortcuts.md).
- **Sources**: [Design Space FAQ](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009503353-Design-Space-FAQ).

### Cricut ID sign-in
- **What**: an account is needed to open any design screen. Signing out needs a
  connection, and signing in again is needed to reach downloaded content.
- **Sources**: [offline feature](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033894014-Design-Space-Offline-Feature). Observed 2026-11-02 in DS 9.83.76: the app would not show a design screen before sign-in.

### Cricut Access
- **What**: a subscription for vendor images, fonts, projects and some tools: Warp,
  Create Sticker, Monogram Maker, automatic background removal, and extra AI credits.
- **Sources**: [free and purchased content](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009552733-How-to-find-free-and-purchased-content-in-Design-Space), [Warp](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/11736800185623-How-to-use-Warp-in-Design-Space), [Create Sticker](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/19366738622359-How-to-use-Create-Sticker-in-Design-Space), [Monogram Maker](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/6143263107991-Design-Space-Using-Monogram-Maker), [upload images](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009556313-How-to-upload-images-into-Design-Space).

### Cricut Credits (AI)
- **What**: credits spent on each AI generation. Free accounts get a small monthly amount,
  Cricut Access more, and StickerPix registration adds some.
- **Sources**: [Cricut and AI](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/43714290860311-Cricut-and-AI-How-To-Use-It-What-Happens-and-Related-Questions).

### Offline mode
- **What**: design and cut without a connection, using downloaded projects, images and
  fonts. Downloaded free images last 7 days offline, Cricut Access images 30 days.
  Offline, search covers only downloaded content and Collections are unavailable.
- **Availability**: desktop and iOS; not Android. Desktop offline projects sync to the
  cloud on reconnect; iOS offline projects do not sync on their own. Deleting the iOS app
  removes downloads; offloading it keeps them.
- **Sources**: [offline feature](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033894014-Design-Space-Offline-Feature), [offload iOS app](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017580693-How-to-offload-the-Cricut-Design-Space-iOS-app).

### Desktop platforms
- **What**: Windows and Mac. Not Chromebooks, Linux or other Unix systems.
- **Sources**: [download and installation](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009378474-Design-Space-Download-and-Installation-Instructions).

### Clear application cache (desktop)
- **What**: a troubleshooting step that clears the desktop app's cache.
- **Sources**: [clearing cache](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040718434-Design-Space-for-Desktop-Clearing-Application-Cache).
